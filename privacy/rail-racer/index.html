Rail Racer Privacy Policy

Developer and Publisher: Doing Stuff Studios

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Introduction

Doing Stuff Studios respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how Rail Racer handles information, stores application data, and may utilize third-party advertising services in future versions.

Information Collection

The initial ad-free version of Rail Racer does not collect or transmit personal information.

Rail Racer does not require user registration, account creation or authentication.

The initial version does not access precise location, contacts, photographs, microphones, cameras or other sensitive device information.

Local Data Storage

Rail Racer stores limited information on your device to preserve gameplay records and user preferences.

This information includes:

  • Personal-best distance records.
  • Music volume settings.
  • Sound effect volume settings.
  • Selected visual environment or theme.

This information is stored within the application's private storage and is not transmitted to Doing Stuff Studios.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Rail Racer is intended to be supported by advertising in future versions.

Future versions may incorporate Google AdMob to display advertisements. When advertising is enabled, Google and applicable advertising partners may collect and process information including:

  • Advertising and device identifiers.
  • IP addresses and approximate geographic location.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Application and advertisement interactions.
  • Performance and diagnostic information.

This information may be used for advertising delivery, personalization where permitted, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

Non-personalized advertisements may still involve data collection and processing.

The initial ad-free version does not contain these advertising services. This Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect the actual advertising implementation before an advertising-enabled version is distributed.

Additional information regarding Google's privacy practices is available at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps
  • How Google retains data

Data Sharing

The initial ad-free version of Rail Racer does not transmit user information to Doing Stuff Studios or third-party advertising providers.

Future advertising-enabled versions may share information with advertising service providers as described in this policy.

Privacy Choices and Advertising Controls

When advertising is introduced, applicable privacy and consent mechanisms will be provided in accordance with relevant legal requirements and the application's advertising configuration.

Users may also manage their advertising identifier and certain advertising preferences through their Android device settings.

Restricting personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertising-related data processing.

Data Security

Rail Racer stores gameplay records and preferences within Android's private application storage, which uses the operating system's application isolation mechanisms.

While reasonable measures are taken to protect information, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay records and preferences are retained until replaced or deleted.

Users may delete this information by:

  1. Opening Android Settings.
  2. Selecting Apps and then Rail Racer.
  3. Selecting Storage and clearing application data.

Uninstalling the application generally removes its local data, although device migration and operating system behavior may affect retention.

Clearing application data does not necessarily delete information previously processed by third-party services.

Children's and Teenagers' Privacy

Rail Racer is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Doing Stuff Studios does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

When advertising is introduced, appropriate age-related advertising and privacy safeguards will be implemented, including applicable restrictions on personalized advertising for minors.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to Doing Stuff Studios, please contact us so that the matter can be investigated and addressed.

External Platform Services

Google Play and Android may independently process information associated with application installation, platform operation and optional device diagnostics.

These services are governed by Google's applicable privacy policies.

Support and Privacy Inquiries

If you contact Doing Stuff Studios for support, bug reporting or privacy inquiries, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, including your email address, message and any attachments you submit.

This information is used to respond to inquiries, investigate reported issues and provide support.

Correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to address the inquiry, maintain necessary records or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Users may request access to or deletion of personal information held by Doing Stuff Studios by contacting the email address below.

Requests concerning information held independently by third-party service providers may need to be directed to those providers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Doing Stuff Studios may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, advertising services, legal requirements or data handling practices.

The current version of this Privacy Policy will remain available at:

https://doingstuffstudios.github.io/privacy/rail-racer/
