Privacy Statement

Doing Stuff Studios operates this website. This statement explains how information is handled when you visit the site or email the studio.

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in to GitHub. GitHub describes its handling of this information in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Cookies and tracking

The website does not set its own cookies or use browser storage, analytics, tracking or advertising scripts, forms, external fonts, or embedded third-party services. Contact links open your email application.

Email correspondence

When you email Doing Stuff Studios, the studio receives the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. The studio uses this information to respond to general, support, business, and privacy inquiries and to investigate bug reports.

Retention and deletion

Correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to address the inquiry, maintain necessary records or comply with applicable legal obligations. Users may request deletion of personal information held by Doing Stuff Studios by contacting doingstuffstudios@proton.me.

Data security

Doing Stuff Studios takes reasonable steps to protect correspondence from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee complete security.

External links

Links on this website may take you to sites operated by other organizations. Those sites handle information under their own privacy policies.

Privacy rights and inquiries

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information the studio holds about you. Contact doingstuffstudios@proton.me to ask a privacy question or exercise those rights. For requests about information GitHub handles as the hosting provider, use the contact options in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes to this statement

Doing Stuff Studios may update this statement as the website or its practices change. The current version and its effective date will be available on this page.
